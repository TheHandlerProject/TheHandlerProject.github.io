The Mission

Not Just Work.

The Handler Project is a mobile recovery service built for the teams others gave up on. We don't work out of a clinic. We come to you — in your space, on your terms — and we work on both ends of the leash.

"Top class care. Real world results. Zero fluff."

We serve veterans, active units, service dog handlers, working K9 teams, and anyone who needs purpose-driven recovery that actually fits their life. We come to you — and for those who prefer it, we also offer services out of our office space. Human optimization and K9 rehabilitation aren't separate services here — they're one mission.
